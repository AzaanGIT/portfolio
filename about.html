My story

Bridging circuits, silicon & intelligence

I'm K Azaan Ahamed, a final-year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at VIT Vellore (CGPA 8.42). I approach engineering with a problem-driven mindset — connecting hardware fundamentals with practical, deployed solutions across Embedded Systems, VLSI Physical Design, and Machine Learning.

My hands-on foundation started at the embedded board and microcontroller level. I enjoy taking systems from schematics to working physical prototypes — building an autonomous Smart Rover with ultrasonic obstacle avoidance and Bluetooth telemetry, designing a multi-joint Robotic Arm with kinematic servo actuation, and developing an Intelligent IV Therapy Monitoring System (IDF patent document) with ESP8266 Wi-Fi alerts to safeguard patients in healthcare environments.

At the chip level, my training and internship at Maven Silicon gave me rigorous hands-on exposure to digital IC implementation. I worked through the complete RTL-to-GDSII flow — running logic synthesis, floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis (CTS), routing, and physical verification using Qflow, OpenSTA, and Cadence tools (Virtuoso, Innovus, Genus) to transform Verilog RTL into clean, verified layout.

To make engineering cycles smarter, I explore the intersection of machine learning and EDA. I am currently developing an ML-based routing congestion predictor for an RV32I RISC-V processor, utilizing gradient boosting on a 30,500+ row dataset to anticipate wiring congestion early and suggest layout parameter optimizations before timing-closure bottlenecks occur.

Alongside engineering, I lead two major student organizations. As Photography Head at SAHITI Club, I coordinated visual coverage across 12+ university events for 1,200+ participants. As Outreach Head at VRIKSH Club, I led community engagement initiatives with 50+ members. I also manage digital media and content creation, applying visual design discipline and leadership to everything I build.
