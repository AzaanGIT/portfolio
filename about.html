My story

From circuits to silicon

I'm Azaan, a final-year B.Tech Electronics & Communication Engineering student at VIT Vellore (CGPA 8.47), with a deep focus on VLSI Physical Design. My goal is simple: to turn a netlist into clean, routable, timing-closed silicon that actually tapes out.

My journey into physical design started when I got curious about what happens after you write Verilog. I wanted to understand every stage — synthesis, floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, STA, and DRC/LVS — not just in theory, but with real tools on real designs.

That led me to Maven Silicon for an industry training and internship where I ran full RTL-to-GDS flows using Cadence Innovus and Genus. It also led me to build an ML-powered congestion predictor using a 30,500-row dataset generated from Cadence tool sweeps — because I believe the future of EDA is intelligent.

Outside the terminal, I'm the Photography Head at VIT's SAHITI club — I love capturing light as much as I love timing closure. I'm also Outreach Head at VRIKSH, VIT's sustainability club.
